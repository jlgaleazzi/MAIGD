## Integration

  and cap tests use the camera-frame velocity correctly.
  - M1/M5 (2.6 < 3): a re-entered fighter closes in and parks on screen. It never escapes, so
    there is no loop.
  - M2 (3.25): a trailing fighter falls behind at 0.25 u/s. It reaches EscapeMargin about 12 s
    after leaving the view. Reinforcements are removed only at 20 u, which takes about 80 s
    behind. They are harmless meanwhile: off screen, not counted in the cap, and not firing
    (the gun needs the fighter on screen).
  - M3/M4 (3.9): one cycle (re-enter, overtaken, fall behind, escape) lasts more than 8 s, most
    of it on screen. This is bounded, not an infinite loop.

  first fighter atX is 30 or more and the scroll is held before the first input, so no fighter
  exists before the start. FighterAI itself has no start gate, which is fine.
- **Minimap and the B053 counter:** they read the aircraft list and the tracker, so the chase
  doesn't change them. `MinimapQaTests` and `FighterCounterQaTests` pass.
- **B051 smoke tail:** `SmokeTailPoint` uses `_direction`, which flips at the turn's zero
  crossing. Test added.

## Attack slots (B060)
- Cap of 3, nearest-first, no pre-emption, grace rotation after 0.5 s, and the hand-over gap
  `lastShot + fighterIntervalMin`: OK by reading the code, and covered by the 16 B060 tests.
- Reset paths: ReleaseAll on the player's death, BindTarget on a new player (Retry/Next),
  `ResetStatics` at play start (test added), plus pruning of destroyed and silent holders.
- No static leaks:
  - EditMode fixtures that fire (EnemyFire, PlanePitch) create a new player per test, so
    BindTarget resets the slots.
  - The B060 and QA fixtures reset `Shared` in SetUp and TearDown.
  - `s_live` stays empty in EditMode unless a test calls OnEnable; the QA fixture calls
    OnDisable on those fighters in TearDown.
- Starvation: waiting fighters fire only when a holder loses its aim, dies or re-enters. Three
  holders parked nose-on keep their slots for as long as they can shoot. That is by design: the
  cap is the point.

## Balance on paper
Worst sustained rate: 3 slots, 1 shot per 1.8 s, 3.33% per hit (5 / resistance 1.5) =
**5.56 %/s**. From full health, every shot hitting, the crash comes after 16.2 s.

| Mission | Cruise vs fighter | Duration | Balloons (30%) | Budget (100 + heals) | Sustainable rate | Worst / sustainable |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| M1 | 2.6 < 3 (can't outrun) | 115 s | 6 | 280% | 2.43 %/s | 2.3x |
| M2 | 3.25 > 3 | 138 s | 7 | 310% | 2.25 %/s | 2.5x |
| M3 | 3.9 > 3 | 128 s | 8 | 340% | 2.66 %/s | 2.1x |
| M4 | 3.9 > 3 | 154 s | 9 | 370% | 2.40 %/s | 2.3x |
| M5 | 2.6 < 3 (can't outrun) | 223 s | 10 | 400% | 1.79 %/s | **3.1x** |

No mission is unsurvivable on paper, so there is no P1 here. The worst case needs 3 noses on
the player continuously and every bullet hitting. Bullets fly at 8 u/s with 4° of spread, and the
player climbs at 6.5 u/s against the fighters' 2.5. Fighters parked ahead in M1/M5 die in about
0.4 s of the player's 8 shots/s. M5 has the thinnest margin: the player must keep the fighter
hit rate under about 32% of the maximum over 223 s, with zeppelin turrets on top. Playtest it
(P2-5).
