# T975 verify: B074 (enemy aircraft enter only from the right edge)

**Verdict: FAIL.** There is 1 P1 bug and 3 P2 bugs. The entry-side rule itself passes: every
creation path places aircraft past the right edge in all 5 missions and both facings. The P1 is
the maxLiveFighters cap. While the player flies left in a mission that cruises faster than a
fighter, reinforcements pile up just behind the right edge. A turn to the right then puts up to
18 fighters on screen at once.

## Bugs

```
BUG: Reinforcements pile up behind a left-flying player; turning right brings 11-18 fighters on screen (cap 6 bypassed)
PRIORITY: P1 mechanic wrong vs GDD
OWNER: mechanics
WHERE: Biplanes/Assets/Biplanes/Scripts/Missions/EnemyPressure.cs:191-195 (reinforcement margin DespawnBehindMargin 20 on the right) with CountLiveFightersNear/CountsTowardCap:295-300
STEPS: M2 (scroll 3.25) or M3/M4 (3.9), take off, turn left and hold for 60 s without shooting, then turn right. Every wave enters on the right (B074), is on screen 1-5 s, then falls back off the right edge at (scroll - 3) u/s, i.e. 0.25 or 0.9 u/s. It is not closing, so it does not count toward maxLiveFighters, and it is only removed 20 u past the edge, which takes 22-80 s. Meanwhile the 2 s rule keeps sending waves. Reproduced by EditMode test EnemyEntrySideQaTests.FlyingLeftThenTurningRight_NeverMoreThanMaxLiveFightersOnScreen (M2: 18 on screen at once) and by a Python model (M3/M4: 11-13).
EXPECTED: GDD "Enemy pressure": a "small wave" of reinforcements; Balance pressure.maxLiveFighters 6 (ARCHITECTURE "Enemy pressure" cap).
ACTUAL: The lingering reinforcements return together (closing at about 6 u/s) after the turn: 18 fighters on screen at once in M2.
FIX IDEA: remove a reinforcement that is wholly off the RIGHT edge and not closing at EscapeMargin (3) instead of 20, or count non-closing fighters within the cap range toward the cap.
```
