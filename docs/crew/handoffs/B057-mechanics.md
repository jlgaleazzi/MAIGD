SUMMARY:
- Cause: `PlayerPlane._velocity` is already the WORLD velocity, (target - start) / dt, with the camera ride included. In steady flight facing left it is (-scrollSpeed, vy), so a bomb went left. The bug is in the second or so after a turn. Facing right, the plane sits at the trailing anchor at viewport 0.3. Turning left moves the anchor to viewport 0.7, so the plane slides RIGHT relative to the camera at player.speed 6.5 to cover 0.4 x 17.8 = 7.1 u, which takes 1.10 s. That slide always runs opposite to the new facing. Meanwhile the camera eases from +s to -s over turnEaseSeconds 0.6. Worked example with scrollSpeed 2.6 (V014): the plane's world vx is +2.6 + 6.5 = +9.1 just after the turn, +3.9 at 0.6 s, and stays +3.9 until the slide ends at 1.10 s. So a bomb dropped in the ~1.1 s after turning left flew RIGHT. From 4 u up (0.89 s fall, drag 0.15), the throw was 7.6 u right just after the turn and 3.25 u right at 0.6 s. A steady-flight throw is 2.17 u left. Players turn toward a target and then bomb, so they hit exactly this window. (B056's handoff gave this slide as "scroll + 6.5" forward. The sign was wrong: the slide is backward.)
- Rule (decided): bomb velocity = `BombBallistics.InheritedVelocity(worldVel, Facing, cruise, inheritFactor)`. y = world vy (climb or dive). x = Facing * max(Facing * worldVx, cruise), where cruise = `ScrollController.ScrollSpeed` (the plane's forward airspeed), or 0 when there is no ScrollController. The whole vector is then multiplied by inheritFactor (a negative factor counts as 0). The screen slide is a camera artefact, not airspeed, so during the turn ease and slide the bomb leaves forward along Facing at the cruise speed. It never travels against the facing. In steady flight worldVx = Facing * cruise, so the bomb gets the plane's exact world velocity and both directions throw the same distance.
- Sprite: unchanged. Bomb.ApplyRotation sets z = atan2(v), with flipY when vx < 0. A test confirms that a bomb spawned facing left points left (z = 180) with flipY set.
