SUMMARY:
- Every aircraft enters past the RIGHT edge of the view and flies left, whatever the player's facing or scroll direction: JSON fighters and bombers use `EnemyPressure.EntryX(camX, halfW, PlaneEntryMarginX)`, reinforcements and re-entries use `EntryX(camX, halfW, EntryOffset(i, rand))` with `EntryFlightDirection = -1`. Zeppelins stay at atX, and `MissionRunner.SpawnTriggerX` keeps the hull off the right edge. `PlayerFacing` no longer picks a side.
- A fighter wholly past the left edge (`HasLeftView`: centre < view.xMin - `LeftExitMargin` 1.0) has escaped at once, even when it is closing. It goes through the unchanged `DecideEscape`: a JSON fighter that an open destroy-all needs re-enters from the right, and any other fighter is removed. So nothing ever comes back in from the left. This also applies to escorts that left formation to chase. Escorts in formation stay exempt.
- When the player faces left and the camera scrolls left, spawns still use the screen's right edge, so fighters come in behind the player. `FighterAI.BeginEntry(camVelX)` adds the camera's leftward speed to the fighter's top speed (`EntryBoost`) until its hitbox first overlaps the view (`EntryComplete`), so it closes at cruise speed in the camera's frame. Plane 0 is on screen within 0.2 s in all missions and both facings. Without the boost, M2-M4 (3.25-3.9 > fighter 3) entries would never reach the view.
