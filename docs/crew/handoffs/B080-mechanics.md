SUMMARY:
Defense wave: when the last turret dies, ZeppelinAI raises the static ShieldDropped event (once per zeppelin, never after death/despawn). MissionRunner queues zeppelinDefense.waveCount (4) fighters for it. They are escorts of the zeppelin (pattern "escort", escortOf = its id), named fighterReinforcement, enter from the right with the entry boost, and are sent one per staggerSeconds (0.6 s) while the fighters near the view are under pressure.maxLiveFighters. At the cap the rest stay queued and go as slots free. Leftovers are dropped once the zeppelin is dead or gone, and on mission end or teardown.
Explosion: Die still raises EntityDestroyed at the killing hit. Then a 1.8 s sequence plays: 12 envelope bursts walking out from the centre to ±3.4 units, plus 3 bursts on the tail and gondolas 1 and 3, while the hull drifts on, sinks, tilts 6° nose-down and fades over the last 0.4 s, then is removed. ExplosionBig plays at the hit, with Explosion layered on every 3rd envelope burst. There is no camera shake: no helper exists, and ScrollController moves the camera from its own position in FixedUpdate, so a shake would need a change in ScrollController. The hull does not fall like the bomber wreck; it sinks and fades in place, which is cheaper.
