SUMMARY: A fighter fires only while it holds one of `fighterChase.maxAttackers` (3) slots. Every gun tick it reports whether it could shoot now (on screen, not turning, player in cone and range, nose within aimTolerance) and its distance. Free slots go to the nearest eligible fighters. A holder is not pre-empted while it can shoot. Slots are freed on death/OnDisable, ReEnter, more than `slotGraceSeconds` (0.5) unable to shoot (this is the rotation, so no starvation), a destroyed or silent holder (pruned), a dead or crashed player (ReleaseAll), and a new player plane (BindTarget resets everything for Next/Retry; ResetStatics resets on play start). Each slot remembers its last shot, and a fighter taking over a slot waits until fighterIntervalMin after it, so handing slots round can't raise the fire rate. Fighters without a slot keep chasing and aiming. Each fighter picks its standoff once, uniformly in [standoff 3.5, standoffMax 6].
Worst-case damage, pack of 6 behind the player in M1/M5 (3.33% per hit; 30 hits = crash): now at most 3 shots per 1.8 s = 1.67 shots/s = 5.56%/s, so the crash comes no sooner than 16.2 s from full health (10 volleys of 3 at 0, 1.8, ... 16.2 s, every shot hitting). At the mean interval of 2.15 s it is 4.65%/s, a crash in about 19-21 s. Before (B059, 6 shooters) it was 11.1%/s and a crash in 7.2-9 s.
