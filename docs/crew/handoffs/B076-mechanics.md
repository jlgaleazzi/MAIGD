SUMMARY: The new EnemyHealthBar is a visual-only child of the bomber (1.6 x 0.12, 1.0 above) and the zeppelin hull (3.0 x 0.16, 1.5 above). It has a pencil frame, a paper backing and a left-anchored ink fill showing the REMAINING fraction (`FillFraction(dmg) = Clamp01(1 - dmg/100)`, NaN = 1). It is always shown while alive. It is drawn from one generated 1x1 sprite at sortingOrder 11/12/13, with no collider, no Rigidbody2D and no events.
Each LateUpdate it cancels the parent's pitch, rotation and mirror (`CancelAngle` + `CompensateScale`, checked against the real world matrix) and stays clamped under the screen top. That keeps a zeppelin clamped near the top edge readable.
It hides as soon as the bound dead check is true. Bomber: Health.IsDead. Zeppelin: IsDead or _destroyed. It is removed together with its parent, so the BomberWreck has none, and teardown, restart and despawn need no bookkeeping. The zeppelin bar shows HULL damage only, so it stays full through the turret phase.
